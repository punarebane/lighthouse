# Lighthouse — itch.io page

**Tagline:** You are the last lighthouse. Hold back the night sea for fifteen minutes.

## Short description
Things crawl out of the dark water toward your rock. You can't move. You can only aim the light.

Sweep a beam of light across the night sea and burn back eels, crabs, splitting jellies, shielded ships, spitters and burrowers. Narrow the beam to burn hard, widen it to cover more. Gather the embers they leave behind, but only the ones your light touches drift home. Level up, pick from 20 upgrades (rear lamps, harpoon turrets, chain lightning, lantern drones, fog horns…), max out the right pairs for four evolutions, and survive three bosses until dawn.

## Controls
| | Mouse & keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Aim | Mouse, or A / D | Right stick (left stick turns) |
| Focus (narrow / wide) | Mouse wheel, or W / S | RT / LT |
| Pause | Esc or P | Start |
| Pick upgrade | Click, or 1 / 2 / 3 | D-pad + A |

## Features
- 8 enemy types and 3 bosses (5:00, 10:00, 15:00)
- 20 upgrades with 5 levels each, plus 4 evolutions
- 8 permanent keeper's upgrades bought with coins between runs
- Real darkness: enemies outside your light are only eyes in the night
- Colour-blind palette, screen-shake slider, CRT vignette, volume controls
- Fully playable with a gamepad
- Synthesised sound, no downloads: about 200 KB
